Color name attributions
=======================

The dcc-rpc plugins display a color name when the user paints with a brush.
Two name sources are bundled. Per their licenses / public-domain status,
neither requires distribution of the underlying license text, but linking
the upstream source is the polite thing to do and so it is preserved here.

Evocative color names (palette.csv)
-----------------------------------
Source:  meodai/color-names
URL:     https://github.com/meodai/color-names
The "best of" CSV (colornames.bestof.csv) is the upstream curated subset.

ISCC-NBS centroid color names (iscc-nbs.csv)
--------------------------------------------
Source:  W3Schools
URL:     https://www.w3schools.com/colors/colors_nbs.asp
The underlying naming system is the ISCC-NBS Centroid Color Chart developed
by the Inter-Society Color Council and the U.S. National Bureau of Standards
(now NIST). Names and centroid colors are in the public domain in the United
States as a U.S. Government work.
